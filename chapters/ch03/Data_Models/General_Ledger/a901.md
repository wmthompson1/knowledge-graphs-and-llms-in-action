** The difference between the last two distributions will be what posts to the general ledger.**
KB0738580
--a902


The detail tables are the only table that can show history in VISUAL. The detail records are created when the bottom portion of costing is run (Manufacturing journal preparation). The detail records will hold the value of the transaction when it occurs.  If that cost changes, costing will insert a new record for the same transaction with a new distribution and the new value. The difference between the last two distributions will be what posts to the general ledger.
