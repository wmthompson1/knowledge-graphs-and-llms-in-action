### What are the different values for Purchase Requisition Status ###

Description :

Purchase Requisition Status


Resolution :
There is a Status field on the header and the line(s).  As a Purchase Requisition can have multiple lines, the status of the lines and header could differ.
Only once all the lines have a particular status will the status change on the header.

Listed below are the statuses:

T = Draft                   
I = In Process            
V = Approved             
O = Order                  
C = Closed                   
X = Cancelled
